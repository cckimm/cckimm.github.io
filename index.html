Item 1 — Research

Research

My research examines how corporate disclosures are shaped by the incentives of the people who make them. Firms communicate through many channels — press conferences, 8-K filings, press releases, and increasingly social media — and I study how managers choose among them, what they choose to amplify, and what that means for investors and the quality of information in markets.
